FAQ

Why wasn't my class imported?
GoRox reads workouts recorded by Apple Watch from Apple Health. Go to Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › GoRox, turn on all read permissions, and make sure you started a workout on your watch during class.

Why wasn't my whiteboard plan recognized?
GoRox looks for photos taken around the time of your workout. Allow access to all photos in Settings › Apps › GoRox › Photos, or take a photo or describe the plan by voice in the app.

I can't find GoRox on my watch.
Open the Watch app on your iPhone, find GoRox and install it on your watch.

How do I delete my data?
In the app, open Profile and choose "Delete Account" ("Delete All Data" if you never signed in). All your data on the server is deleted, and if you signed in, Sign in with Apple is revoked. Deleting the app alone does not delete data on the server.
