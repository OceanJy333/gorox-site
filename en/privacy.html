GoRox Privacy Policy

Updated: November 10, 2026　Effective: November 10, 2026　·　中文

In short: You can use GoRox without an account; if you sign in with Apple, your data stays when you change phones. Workout data is uploaded to the GoRox server, where it is split, scored and used for predictions. To read class plans, write workout summaries and read race result screenshots, the server sends the needed content to an AI model service. You can delete your account and all data in the app at any time.

1. Who we are

GoRox is run by an independent developer for people who do HYROX and fitness classes with Apple Watch. For privacy questions and data requests, email oceanjy@outlook.com.

2. Data the app reads

  • Health and fitness data: with your permission, workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, distance and active energy from Apple Health, plus sex, age, height and weight (to prefill your profile); and sleep, heart rate variability, respiratory rate and wrist temperature, used to calculate recovery and sleep.
  • Watch workouts: the Apple Watch app reads your heart rate and active energy during a workout and saves the segment-by-segment workout to Apple Health.
  • Photos: with your permission, whiteboard workout photos taken around your classes; you can also take a photo of the plan with the camera.
  • Voice: when you describe a plan by voice, the microphone is used and the system's speech recognition turns speech into text.
  • Content you enter: your profile (name and photo are optional), your real name (optional, used to find your results in official public race results), class plans, race goals and results.
  • Account and device: without signing in, the app creates an anonymous account. With Sign in with Apple, we receive the anonymous user identifier Apple provides. The app generates a device identifier used to track the free trial period. If you allow notifications, we store this device's push token.
  • Subscription: when you subscribe through the App Store, the server stores the transaction record Apple provides (product and expiry date) to confirm your membership.

3. Data that leaves your phone

Uploaded to the GoRox server: after onboarding explains what the data is used for, the app uploads the health and fitness data listed above, text recognized in photos, class plan or result photos when they need to be read, and content you enter. The server is located in mainland China and uses this data to calculate your workout splits, scores, station abilities and race predictions.

Sent to an AI model service: when you use plan reading, workout summaries or result screenshot import, the GoRox server sends the following through OpenRouter to a model provider in the United States to return recognition or summary results:

  • Reading a class plan: whiteboard or plan photos, and text from your voice description.
  • Workout summary: the class's segment times, completed work, heart rate summary, intensity and performance scores.
  • Importing race results: the result screenshot you choose.

Partner: after you pair with a doubles partner, they can see your name, photo, rank and race prediction, and doubles race plans and results are shared with them.

We do not use this data for advertising, sell it or build profiles, and we do not share HealthKit data with any advertising or data broker. OpenRouter and its model providers handle requests under their own privacy policies.

4. Storage and deletion

Workout records, analysis results and your profile are kept on the GoRox server until you delete your account. Plan and result photos are kept on the GoRox server to improve recognition for up to 12 months, and are deleted when you delete your account. In the app, open Profile and choose "Delete Account" ("Delete All Data" if you never signed in): the server deletes all your data and, if you signed in, revokes Sign in with Apple. After deletion only the device identifier is kept, to prevent the free trial from being claimed again. You can also withdraw Health, Photos, Camera, Microphone and Speech Recognition access in iOS Settings at any time.

5. Children

GoRox is not intended for anyone under 16.

6. Health notice

GoRox is a training log and analysis tool. It is not a medical device and does not give diagnosis or treatment advice. If you feel unwell, see a doctor.

7. Changes

We will update this page when features or data processing change in a material way.
